# Broadway Betties promotion and sponsorship slots

The four `data-promo-slot` banners are reusable. For now, every banner promotes audience participation and links to the Get Featured form. Later, any one of them can become a paid sponsor placement without changing the page structure.

| Slot | Location | Current message | Current action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `participation-iconic` | After the introduction | We want to make you iconic. | Get featured |
| `participation-famous` | After What's Poppin' | Let's make you famous. | Step into the spotlight |
| `participation-pose` | After Betties Fit Check | Show us your hottest Betties pose. | Strike a pose |
| `participation-energy` | Before Get Featured | We want to see your main character energy. | Show us your energy |

For a future sponsor, replace the label, headline, link text and destination inside one banner. Keep the `data-promo-slot` value so its location remains easy to identify and measure.
